# Being on a jury

[FullFrame user guide](README.md) › Being on a jury

A curator has asked you to score the photographs of an exhibition. You don't
need an account: open the **personal link** the curator sent you. It's yours
alone, so don't share it.

If judging hasn't opened yet, the page says so. Your link stays valid, so come
back to it later.

## Scoring

The jury room shows one photograph at a time, with its title and its position
(*1 / 3*).

![Starting to score](images/jurors-scoring-start.png)

For each photograph:

1. Choose a score for **Overall impression**, from **1** (*less compelling*) to
   **5** (*exceptional*).
2. Optionally write a **private note**: something worth remembering. Other
   jurors and visitors never see it. The curator receives it with the jury
   record.
3. Click **Next photograph →**.

Scores and notes **save automatically**. The line under the note says *Saving…*,
then *Saved*. The bar at the top counts how many you've scored.

![Scoring a photograph, with a note](images/jurors-scoring.png)

You can go back with **← Previous**, or open **View all photographs** to jump to
any of them. The overview marks which ones you've scored and which are still to
score.

![Your progress](images/jurors-progress.png)

You can change any score while judging is open. After the last photograph,
**Review scores →** brings up the overview.

## When judging closes

When the curator closes judging, your scores become read-only and the page thanks
you. **Leave jury** at the top right ends your session. To come back, open your
personal link again.

The jury room appears in the exhibition's language, chosen by the curator.
