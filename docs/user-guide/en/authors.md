# Sending your photographs

[FullFrame user guide](README.md) › Sending your photographs

A curator has invited you to send photographs to an exhibition. You don't need
an account. Everything happens through the **personal link** the curator sent
you, so keep it to yourself.

## Opening your link

The page greets you by name and says how many photographs you may send.

![An author's invitation](../images/authors-invited.png)

If it says submissions are closed or not open yet, your link is still valid:
come back when the curator opens them.

## Sending photographs

1. Click **Choose photographs** (or drop files on the box). The page says the
   largest file size accepted.
2. Give each photograph a **title**; it's how visitors and the jury will know
   it. Your name is filled in from the invitation and can't be changed.
   **Technique** (e.g. *Silver gelatin print*), **Dimensions** (e.g.
   *50 × 70 cm*) and **Description** are optional.
3. Click **Add 2 photographs** (the number matches yours).

![Describing two photographs](../images/authors-describe.png)

They're sent straight to the exhibition. **Your photographs** lists what you've
sent.

![Photographs sent](../images/authors-sent.png)

## Correcting or withdrawing

While submissions are open you can come back with the same link and:

- **Edit** a photograph's details;
- **Remove** a photograph (which frees a place to send another).

Once the curator closes submissions, your photographs are fixed.

## Finishing

Click **Finish** at the top right when you're done. You can close the page. The
curator has your photographs.

![Done](../images/authors-finished.png)

The page appears in the exhibition's language, chosen by the curator.
